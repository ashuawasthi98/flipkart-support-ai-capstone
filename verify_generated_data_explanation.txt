Classification: Missing at Random (MAR)

Justification: The missingness pattern in rating_given is classified as Missing at Random (MAR) because the probability of a value being missing depends entirely on another observed variable in the dataset—specifically, payment_method—and does not depend on the unobserved rating value itself (which would make it MNAR), nor is it completely independent of all data (which would make it MCAR).
Looking at the data generator logic in generate_orders.py, the missingness mask is constructed conditionally based on the payment method: missing_mask = rng.random(N) < np.where(payment_method == "COD", 0.22, 0.06).
This conditional probability is clearly reflected in our empirical data verification:
COD orders exhibit a missingness rate of 22.8309%.
Non-COD orders (Prepaid Card, Prepaid UPI, Wallet) exhibit a combined missingness rate of only 6.0589%.
This creates a significant, observed missingness gap of 16.7720%
